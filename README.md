**How to Play**

- Take care of your very own Tabas.
- Enter a number based on the menu to do an action.  
- Each action increases and/or decreases certain stats. *Pay attention!*  
- Your Tabas dies if:
  > - All stats reach 0
  > - Is sick, and hunger OR energy levels reach 0
  > - Gets overdosed
